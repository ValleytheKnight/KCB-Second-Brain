## Decision

Approved a temp/working logo for Scryptable: a quill in brand orange (#FF8400), with an audio waveform line originating from the nib tip as if being actively drawn/written.

## Rationale

Went through several rejected directions first: feather-as-waveform (unreadable as a quill), waveform embedded in the feather following its spine angle (still read wrong), headphones wrapped around the quill (hated more than the waveform attempts), quill+paper+waveform floating near the feather (felt disconnected). Landed on the pen actively drawing the waveform out of its own nib, which reads clearly as both writing and audio/transcription, and matches the app's locked theme color exactly. Marked temp, not final, until formally locked as a design asset.
