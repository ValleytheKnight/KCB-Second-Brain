# Welcome to Your COG Second Brain, Chris!

Your COG is now personalized and ready to use. Here's how to get started.

## Your Profile Documents

I've created these documents to store your preferences:

- **[[MY-PROFILE]]** - Your basic info, role pack, and workflow preferences
- **[[MY-INTERESTS]]** - Topics for your daily briefs
- **[[MY-INTEGRATIONS]]** - Your active and disabled integrations

You can edit these files anytime. COG reads them when you use skills, so your changes take effect immediately.

## Skills for Your Role

You're matched to the **Engineer** role pack (tuned for a self-taught, learning-as-you-go style, so I'll keep explanations simple and avoid assumed jargon):

1. **onboarding** - Initial setup, run again anytime to update your profile
2. **braindump** - Capture technical learnings, debugging insights, and design ideas for Knight Code, your plugins, or the Electron app; auto-classified by domain
3. **daily-brief** - Stay current on LLMs, agentic workflows, MCP, Obsidian dev, and related tools
4. **url-dump** - Save docs, blog posts, and GitHub repos with auto-extracted key takeaways
5. **weekly-checkin** - Reflect on what you shipped, what you learned, and where you want to grow
6. **knowledge-consolidation** - Build personal reference docs from scattered notes and learnings
7. **update-cog** - Keep the COG framework current

## Tracking Project Progress

Each project has a `PROJECT-OVERVIEW.md` with a running "Next Steps" checklist. Recommended approach given you're managing a few in-progress builds at once:
- Keep the checklist short and current, check items off as you go instead of writing new lists each time.
- Use braindump for design decisions, debugging notes, and ideas as they come up; they'll file themselves under the right project.
- Use weekly-checkin to step back and see what actually moved across Knight Code, your plugins/themes, and the Electron app.

## How COG Uses Your Profile

**Daily Briefs**: Uses [[MY-INTERESTS]] to curate relevant news
**Braindumps**: Offers your projects from [[MY-PROFILE]] as options
**Weekly Check-ins**: Reviews progress across your projects

## Keeping COG Updated

COG separates your content from framework files. When new versions are released:
- Run `/update-cog` to check for and apply updates
- Your braindumps, profiles, and notes are never touched by updates

## Tips for Success

- Don't overthink it, just dump your thoughts, COG will help organize
- Be consistent, daily briefs and braindumps work best as habits
- Review weekly with weekly-checkin to see patterns emerge
- Evolve your setup anytime, edit your profile files or run onboarding again to add projects
